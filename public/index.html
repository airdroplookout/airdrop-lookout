Stay safe this week

5-tip checklist

Myth-buster

“A real claim might ask for my seed phrase to verify me.” False — always. No legitimate project, support team or claim page ever needs your seed phrase or private key. The moment a site asks for one, close it.

Red flags this week

  • DM and reply links for Treehouse, Hyperliquid “Season 2” or a MetaMask MASK claim
  • “Claim in 10 minutes or lose it” pressure
  • Being asked to send funds first to “unlock” a reward
  • Lookalike domains — extra letters, hyphens or a different ending

The 30-second rule: start from the official website or verified account — never a DM — read what you are about to sign, and if anything feels rushed, close the tab and check again later.
